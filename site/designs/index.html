Emily Cruz

Five designs

The same facts and photographs, set five ways. Emily chooses one; it becomes the live site. The live site uses design 1 until then.

  1. 1. Sage

    Quiet: the first page, grown up. Sage green, plain sans, photo and words side by side.

  2. 2. Programme

    Editorial: set like a theatre programme. Cream paper, serif type, credits first.

  3. 3. Blue light

    Bold: the deep blue of her showcase stage, amber type, showreels up front.

  4. 4. Contact sheet

    Gallery: the photographs lead, words come after.

  5. 5. Score

    Unexpected: the page read as a piece of music, in numbered bars.
