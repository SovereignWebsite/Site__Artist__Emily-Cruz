  2. 2. Blue lightThe showcase stage: dark first, her portrait lit out of the dark, the showreels as the main act.
  3. 3. Contact sheetA photographer's proof sheet: numbered frames on a film strip, each credit as its label; words after.
  4. 4. ScoreA music score: the instrumental reel as the overture, movements for sections, her skills on staves.
  5. 5. BriefThe plain casting brief: one printable page for a casting director in a hurry.
