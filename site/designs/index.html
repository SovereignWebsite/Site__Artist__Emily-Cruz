Emily Cruz

Five designs

The same facts and photographs, each built around a different idea from Emily's world. Emily chooses one; it becomes the live site. Until then the live site uses design 1, Programme.

  1. 1. Programme

    A theatre programme: a cover with her portrait, the credits as a cast list, the agent on the back page.

  2. 2. Blue light

    The showcase stage: dark first, her portrait lit out of the dark, the showreels as the main act.

  3. 3. Contact sheet

    A photographer's proof sheet: numbered frames on a film strip, each credit as its label; words after.

  4. 4. Score

    A music score: the instrumental reel as the overture, movements for sections, her skills on staves.

  5. 5. Brief

    The plain casting brief: one printable page for a casting director in a hurry.
